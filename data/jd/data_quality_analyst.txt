 Data Quality Analyst
Job Summary:
Ensure accuracy, consistency, and reliability of data used for decision-making in quality and
business processes.
Key Responsibilities:
• Monitor and validate data quality across systems
• Identify data inconsistencies and errors
• Develop data quality rules and standards
• Generate reports and dashboards
• Collaborate with data and IT teams
Required Skills:
• Data analysis and SQL
• Knowledge of data governance principles
• Attention to detail
• Analytical thinking
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Data Science, IT, or related field
• 2–5 years of experience
