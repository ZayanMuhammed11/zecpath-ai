Kaizen Specialist
Job Summary:
Lead Kaizen initiatives to drive incremental improvements in processes, quality, and
productivity.
Key Responsibilities:
• Plan and execute Kaizen events
• Identify small, continuous improvement opportunities
• Engage employees in improvement activities
• Monitor results and sustain improvements
• Promote a culture of continuous improvement
Required Skills:
• Knowledge of Kaizen principles
• Facilitation and team engagement skills
• Problem-solving and analytical thinking
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering or related field
• 3–7 years of experience
