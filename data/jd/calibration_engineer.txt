Calibration Engineer

Job Summary:
Maintain accuracy of measuring instruments through regular calibration.
Key Responsibilities:
• Calibrate measuring and testing equipment
• Maintain calibration records
• Ensure compliance with standards
• Identify faulty instruments
• Coordinate with external calibration agencies
Required Skills:
• Knowledge of calibration standards
• Attention to detail
• Instrument handling skills
Qualifications:
• Diploma/Bachelor’s degree in Engineering
• 0–2 years experience
