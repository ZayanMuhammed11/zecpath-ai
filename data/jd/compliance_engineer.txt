Compliance Engineer
Job Summary:
Ensure that products and processes comply with regulatory and industry standards.
Key Responsibilities:
• Monitor regulatory requirements
• Ensure compliance with standards (ISO, industry-specific)
• Conduct audits and inspections
• Maintain compliance documentation
• Support certification processes
Required Skills:
• Knowledge of regulations and standards
• Attention to detail
• Documentation and auditing skills
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering or related field
• 3–6 years of experience
