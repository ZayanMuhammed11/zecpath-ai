Automotive Quality Engineer
Job Summary:
Ensure that automotive products and components meet quality, safety, and regulatory
standards throughout the manufacturing and product lifecycle.
Key Responsibilities:
• Implement and maintain quality standards specific to the automotive industry
• Conduct process and product audits
• Perform root cause analysis and implement corrective/preventive actions (CAPA)
• Monitor quality metrics such as PPM, scrap, and rework
• Collaborate with production, design, and supplier teams
• Support new product development and launch activities
Required Skills:
• Knowledge of automotive quality standards and tools
• Familiarity with APQP, PPAP, FMEA, and Control Plans
• Problem-solving and analytical skills
• Strong communication and teamwork
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical, Automotive, or related Engineering field
• 3–7 years of experience in automotive manufacturing
