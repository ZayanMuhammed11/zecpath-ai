Validation Engineer
Job Summary:
Ensure that systems, equipment, and processes meet defined requirements and regulatory
standards through structured validation activities.
Key Responsibilities:
• Develop and execute validation protocols (IQ, OQ, PQ)
• Prepare validation plans, reports, and documentation
• Ensure compliance with regulatory and quality standards
• Coordinate with engineering, production, and QA teams
• Identify deviations and implement corrective actions
• Support audits and regulatory inspections
Required Skills:
• Knowledge of validation lifecycle (IQ/OQ/PQ)
• Strong documentation and compliance skills
• Understanding of regulatory standards (GMP, ISO)
• Analytical and problem-solving skills
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering or related field
• 2–6 years of experience
