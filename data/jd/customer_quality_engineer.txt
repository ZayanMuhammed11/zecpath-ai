Customer Quality Engineer
Job Summary:
Act as the interface between customers and internal teams to resolve quality issues.
Key Responsibilities:
• Handle customer complaints and feedback
• Conduct root cause analysis and corrective actions
• Improve customer satisfaction
• Coordinate with internal teams for issue resolution
• Maintain customer quality reports
Required Skills:
• Strong communication skills
• Customer handling and problem-solving
• Analytical thinking
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering
• 3–6 years of experience
