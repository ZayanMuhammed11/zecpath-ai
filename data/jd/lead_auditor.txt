Lead Auditor (ISO 9001 / ISO 14001 /ISO 45001)
Job Summary:
Lead and manage internal and external audits to ensure compliance with international
standards and certification requirements.
Key Responsibilities:
• Plan, lead, and conduct audits as per ISO standards
• Evaluate organizational processes for compliance
• Identify gaps and recommend improvements
• Prepare detailed audit reports and present findings
• Guide teams in implementing corrective actions
• Maintain audit schedules and documentation
Required Skills:
• In-depth knowledge of ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001
• Strong auditing and leadership skills
• Excellent communication and presentation skills
• Analytical and problem-solving abilities
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering or related field
• 4–8 years of experience
• Certified Lead Auditor (ISO standards)
