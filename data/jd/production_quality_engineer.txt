 Production Quality Engineer
Job Summary:
Ensure quality during production by monitoring processes and outputs.
Key Responsibilities:
• Monitor production quality metrics
• Identify and resolve quality issues
• Implement corrective and preventive actions
• Conduct in-process inspections
• Collaborate with production teams
Required Skills:
• Production process knowledge
• Quality control techniques
• Analytical skills
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering
• 2–5 years of experience
