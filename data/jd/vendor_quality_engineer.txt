Vendor Quality Engineer
Job Summary:
Ensure that vendors consistently deliver materials and components that meet quality
standards and specifications.
Key Responsibilities:
• Evaluate vendor quality performance and compliance
• Conduct inspections and audits of vendor facilities
• Investigate and resolve vendor-related quality issues
• Implement corrective and preventive actions (CAPA) with vendors
• Maintain vendor quality documentation and reports
• Collaborate with procurement and supply chain teams
Required Skills:
• Vendor quality management
• Root cause analysis and CAPA
• Auditing and inspection techniques
• Analytical and problem-solving skills
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering
• 3–6 years of experience
