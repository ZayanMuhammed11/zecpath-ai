Supplier Quality Manager
Job Summary:
Oversee supplier quality performance and ensure consistent supply of quality materials.
Key Responsibilities:
• Develop supplier quality strategies
• Conduct supplier audits and evaluations
• Monitor supplier performance metrics
• Lead supplier development programs
• Resolve supplier-related quality issues
Required Skills:
• Supplier management and auditing
• Negotiation and communication skills
• Analytical problem-solving
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering
• 8–12 years of experience
