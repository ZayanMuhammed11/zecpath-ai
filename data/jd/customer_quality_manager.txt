Customer Quality Manager
Job Summary:
Manage customer-facing quality activities to ensure satisfaction and issue resolution.
Key Responsibilities:
• Handle customer complaints and escalations
• Lead root cause analysis and corrective actions
• Improve customer satisfaction metrics
• Coordinate with internal teams for resolution
• Maintain customer quality reports
Required Skills:
• Strong communication and interpersonal skills
• Customer relationship management
• Problem-solving and analytical skills
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering
• 8–12 years of experience
