Product Quality Engineer
Job Summary:
Ensure that products meet quality standards throughout the product lifecycle.
Key Responsibilities:
• Define product quality requirements
• Monitor product performance and reliability
• Handle customer complaints and feedback
• Support new product development (NPD)
• Conduct failure analysis
Required Skills:
• Product lifecycle knowledge
• Problem-solving and analytical skills
• Customer-focused approach
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering
• 2–5 years of experience
