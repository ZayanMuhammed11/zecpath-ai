Inspection Engineer

Job Summary:
Perform detailed inspections of raw materials, in-process items, and finished products.
Key Responsibilities:
• Conduct dimensional and visual inspections
• Use measuring instruments (calipers, micrometers, gauges)
• Prepare inspection reports
• Ensure compliance with specifications
• Support audits and quality checks
Required Skills:
• Proficiency in measurement tools
• Understanding of engineering drawings
• High attention to detail
Qualifications:
• Diploma/Bachelor’s in Mechanical or related field
• 0–2 years experience
