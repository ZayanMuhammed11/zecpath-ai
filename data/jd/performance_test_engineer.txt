Performance Test Engineer
Job Summary:
Evaluate system performance, scalability, and stability under various load conditions.
Key Responsibilities:
• Design and execute performance, load, and stress tests
• Analyze system performance metrics
• Identify bottlenecks and recommend improvements
• Work with development teams to optimize performance
• Prepare performance test reports
Required Skills:
• Performance testing tools (JMeter, LoadRunner, Gatling)
• Understanding of system architecture and scalability
• Data analysis and monitoring tools
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or related field
• 2–6 years of experience
