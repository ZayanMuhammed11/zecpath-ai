AI-Based Quality Engineer
Job Summary:
Apply artificial intelligence and machine learning techniques to improve quality prediction,
defect detection, and process optimization.
Key Responsibilities:
• Develop AI/ML models for defect detection and quality prediction
• Analyze large datasets to identify quality trends and patterns
• Implement computer vision systems for automated inspection
• Collaborate with data science and engineering teams
• Optimize processes using predictive analytics
Required Skills:
• Machine learning and AI concepts
• Programming (Python, R)
• Data analysis and modeling
• Knowledge of computer vision tools
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s/Master’s degree in Engineering, Data Science, or related field
• 3–8 years of experience
