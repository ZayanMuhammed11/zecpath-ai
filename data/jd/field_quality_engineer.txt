Field Quality Engineer
Job Summary:
Address quality issues at customer sites and ensure product performance in the field.
Key Responsibilities:
• Investigate field failures
• Support customers with technical issues
• Conduct root cause analysis
• Provide feedback to internal teams
• Improve product quality based on field data
Required Skills:
• Troubleshooting and problem-solving
• Customer interaction skills
• Technical knowledge of products
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering
• 3–6 years of experience
