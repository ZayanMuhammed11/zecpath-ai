Industrial Quality Engineer
Job Summary:
Focus on improving quality across industrial operations by optimizing systems, processes,
and workflows.
Key Responsibilities:
• Analyze and improve industrial processes for quality and efficiency
• Implement quality standards and continuous improvement practices
• Conduct system and process audits
• Identify process inefficiencies and implement corrective actions
• Support Lean manufacturing and Six Sigma initiatives
Required Skills:
• Industrial engineering concepts
• Process optimization and workflow analysis
• Lean and Six Sigma methodologies
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Industrial Engineering or related field
• 2–6 years of experience
