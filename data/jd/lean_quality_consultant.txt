Lean Quality Consultant
Job Summary:
Help organizations eliminate waste and improve quality using Lean principles and tools.
Key Responsibilities:
• Implement Lean methodologies (5S, VSM, Kaizen)
• Identify and eliminate non-value-added activities
• Improve process flow and efficiency
• Train teams on Lean practices
• Support continuous improvement initiatives
Required Skills:
• Lean manufacturing expertise
• Process mapping and optimization
• Change management skills
• Strong analytical thinking
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering or related field
• Lean certification preferred
• 8–15 years of experience
