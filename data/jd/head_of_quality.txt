Head of Quality
Job Summary:
Lead the organization’s overall quality strategy and ensure alignment with business
objectives.
Key Responsibilities:
• Define quality vision and strategy
• Oversee all quality functions and teams
• Ensure compliance with global standards
• Drive a culture of quality excellence
• Report quality performance to senior leadership
Required Skills:
• Strategic leadership
• Strong decision-making and communication
• Deep knowledge of quality systems
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s/Master’s degree in Engineering or Management
• 12–18 years of experience
