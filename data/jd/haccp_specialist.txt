HACCP Specialist
Job Summary:
Develop, implement, and maintain Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points (HACCP)
systems to ensure food safety and compliance.
Key Responsibilities:
• Conduct hazard analysis and identify critical control points
• Develop and maintain HACCP plans and documentation
• Monitor critical control points and ensure compliance
• Conduct food safety audits and inspections
• Train employees on HACCP and food safety practices
• Investigate food safety incidents and implement corrective actions
• Ensure compliance with regulatory and customer requirements
Required Skills:
• In-depth knowledge of HACCP principles
• Food safety and risk assessment expertise
• Auditing and documentation skills
• Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Food Science, Microbiology, or related field
• HACCP certification required
• 3–7 years of experience
