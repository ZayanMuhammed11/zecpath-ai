Manual Test Engineer
Job Summary:
Execute manual testing to ensure software applications meet functional and user
requirements.
Key Responsibilities:
• Create and execute manual test cases
• Perform functional, regression, and exploratory testing
• Identify, document, and report bugs
• Validate fixes and perform re-testing
• Collaborate with development and product teams
Required Skills:
• Strong understanding of manual testing techniques
• Attention to detail
• Knowledge of test case design
• Familiarity with bug tracking tools
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, IT, or related field
• 1–4 years of experience
