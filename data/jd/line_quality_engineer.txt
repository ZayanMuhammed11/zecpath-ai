Line Quality Engineer
Job Summary:
Oversee quality on specific production lines to ensure defect-free output and process
consistency.
Key Responsibilities:
• Monitor quality performance on assigned production lines
• Conduct line audits and inspections
• Identify and resolve line-specific quality issues
• Implement process improvements to reduce defects
• Coordinate with operators and supervisors
Required Skills:
• Shop floor and line management knowledge
• Root cause analysis
• Communication and coordination skills
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering
• 2–5 years of experience
