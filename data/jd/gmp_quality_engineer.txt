GMP Quality Engineer
Job Summary:
Ensure that manufacturing processes and products comply with Good Manufacturing
Practices (GMP) and regulatory requirements in pharmaceutical and healthcare environments.
Key Responsibilities:
• Implement and maintain GMP-compliant quality systems
• Monitor manufacturing processes for adherence to GMP standards
• Conduct internal audits and inspections
• Review batch records, deviations, and change controls
• Support regulatory audits and inspections
• Drive corrective and preventive actions (CAPA)
Required Skills:
• Strong knowledge of GMP regulations and guidelines
• Quality systems and documentation control
• Auditing and compliance skills
• Attention to detail and analytical thinking
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Pharmacy, Biotechnology, Chemical Engineering, or related
field
• 3–7 years of experience in pharmaceutical or healthcare industry
