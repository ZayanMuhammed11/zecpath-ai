Digital Quality Engineer
Job Summary:
Leverage digital technologies to enhance quality management systems, automate quality
processes, and improve real-time decision-making.
Key Responsibilities:
• Implement digital quality tools and platforms (QMS software, dashboards)
• Automate quality data collection and reporting
• Integrate quality systems with ERP/MES platforms
• Monitor real-time quality metrics and KPIs
• Drive digital transformation initiatives in quality processes
• Collaborate with IT and operations teams
Required Skills:
• Knowledge of digital tools (MES, ERP, QMS software)
• Data analysis and visualization (Power BI, Tableau)
• Understanding of manufacturing and quality systems
• Basic programming or scripting knowledge
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, IT, or related field
• 3–7 years of experience
