PPAP Engineer (Production Part
Approval Process)
Job Summary:
Manage and execute the Production Part Approval Process (PPAP) to ensure that suppliers
and internal processes consistently meet customer requirements.
Key Responsibilities:
• Prepare and review PPAP documentation (PSW, control plans, FMEA, process flow
diagrams)
• Coordinate PPAP submissions with customers and suppliers
• Ensure compliance with customer-specific requirements
• Validate production processes and product samples
• Track and resolve PPAP-related issues
• Support new product launches and engineering changes
Required Skills:
• Strong knowledge of PPAP and APQP processes
• Documentation and compliance expertise
• Attention to detail
• Communication and coordination skills
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering
• 3–6 years of experience in automotive or manufacturing industry
