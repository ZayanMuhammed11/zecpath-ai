QA/QC Consultant
Job Summary:
Advise organizations on establishing and improving Quality Assurance and Quality Control
processes.
Key Responsibilities:
• Design QA/QC processes and inspection frameworks
• Evaluate existing QA/QC systems and recommend improvements
• Conduct audits and compliance checks
• Develop quality documentation and SOPs
• Train teams on QA/QC practices
Required Skills:
• Expertise in QA and QC methodologies
• Inspection and auditing knowledge
• Documentation and process design skills
• Attention to detail
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering or related field
• 8–15 years of experience
