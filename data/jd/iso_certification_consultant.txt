ISO Certification Consultant
Job Summary:
Guide organizations through ISO certification processes and ensure compliance with
international standards.
Key Responsibilities:
• Conduct gap analysis for ISO standards (ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, etc.)
• Develop and implement required documentation and processes
• Prepare organizations for certification audits
• Train employees on ISO requirements
• Support audit closure and certification achievement
Required Skills:
• In-depth knowledge of ISO standards
• Documentation and auditing expertise
• Training and consulting skills
• Attention to detail
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering or related field
• Lead Auditor certification preferred
• 8–15 years of experience
