Vice President (VP) – Quality
Job Summary:
Drive enterprise-wide quality strategy, governance, and performance at the executive level.
Key Responsibilities:
• Define and lead global quality strategy
• Establish governance frameworks and policies
• Oversee all quality functions across regions/business units
• Drive organizational excellence and customer satisfaction
• Represent quality at executive and board levels
Required Skills:
• Executive leadership and vision
• Strong strategic and analytical thinking
• Expertise in global quality standards and compliance
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s/Master’s degree in Engineering, Business, or related field
• 18–25+ years of experience
