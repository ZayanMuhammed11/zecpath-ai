Process Quality Engineer (Junior)

Job Summary:
Monitor and improve quality within manufacturing processes.
Key Responsibilities:
• Analyze process performance
• Identify quality issues in production
• Implement corrective and preventive actions
• Support process improvement initiatives
• Monitor KPIs and quality metrics
Required Skills:
• Basic understanding of process control
• Knowledge of quality tools (Pareto, Fishbone, etc.)
• Analytical thinking
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering
• 0–2 years experience
