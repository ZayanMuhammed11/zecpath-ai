AS9100 Quality Specialist
Job Summary:
Ensure the organization’s Quality Management System complies with AS9100 standards and
supports certification and continuous improvement.
Key Responsibilities:
• Implement and maintain AS9100 Quality Management System (QMS)
• Conduct internal audits and gap assessments
• Ensure compliance with AS9100 requirements across processes
• Manage documentation and quality records
• Support external audits and certification processes
• Train employees on quality standards and procedures
• Drive continuous improvement initiatives
Required Skills:
• In-depth knowledge of AS9100 and ISO 9001 standards
• Auditing and documentation control
• Process improvement and analytical skills
• Strong attention to detail
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering or related field
• 4–8 years of experience in quality systems
• AS9100 Internal/Lead Auditor certification preferred
