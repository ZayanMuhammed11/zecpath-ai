Process Improvement Consultant
Job Summary:
Analyze and optimize business and manufacturing processes to improve quality, efficiency,
and performance.
Key Responsibilities:
• Evaluate current processes and identify inefficiencies
• Design and implement improved workflows
• Use data analysis to support decision-making
• Lead improvement projects
• Monitor and sustain process improvements
Required Skills:
• Process optimization and analysis
• Data-driven problem-solving
• Project management
• Communication and stakeholder management
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s/Master’s degree in Engineering or Management
• 8–15 years of experience
