IATF 16949 Quality Engineer
Job Summary:
Ensure compliance with IATF 16949 quality management system requirements within
automotive manufacturing operations.
Key Responsibilities:
• Implement and maintain IATF 16949 standards
• Conduct internal audits and gap assessments
• Ensure process compliance and documentation control
• Support certification and surveillance audits
• Drive continuous improvement initiatives aligned with IATF requirements
• Train teams on quality standards and procedures
Required Skills:
• In-depth knowledge of IATF 16949 and ISO 9001
• Auditing and documentation skills
• Process improvement and analytical thinking
• Strong attention to detail
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering or related field
• 4–8 years of experience in automotive quality
• Internal/Lead Auditor certification preferred
