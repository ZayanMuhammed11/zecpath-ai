Predictive Quality Engineer
Job Summary:
Use predictive analytics to forecast quality issues and prevent defects before they occur.
Key Responsibilities:
• Develop predictive models for quality and defect prevention
• Analyze historical and real-time data
• Identify risk patterns and failure trends
• Implement predictive maintenance and quality strategies
• Collaborate with data science and operations teams
Required Skills:
• Predictive analytics and machine learning
• Data modeling and statistical analysis
• Programming (Python, R)
• Strong analytical thinking
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s/Master’s degree in Engineering, Data Science, or related field
• 3–8 years of experience
