Testing Engineer

Job Summary:
Execute tests on products or systems to verify performance and quality.
Key Responsibilities:
• Develop and execute test plans
• Perform functional and performance testing
• Record and analyze test results
• Report defects and issues
• Collaborate with design and production teams
Required Skills:
• Knowledge of testing methodologies
• Analytical thinking
• Basic troubleshooting skills
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering
• 0–2 years experience
