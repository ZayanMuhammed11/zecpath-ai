Director of Quality
Job Summary:
Provide strategic direction for quality across the organization and ensure long-term
excellence.
Key Responsibilities:
• Develop and execute quality strategies
• Lead large-scale quality transformation initiatives
• Manage multiple quality teams and functions
• Ensure compliance with industry regulations
• Align quality goals with business objectives
Required Skills:
• Strategic planning and leadership
• Change management expertise
• Strong business acumen
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s/Master’s degree in Engineering or Management
• 15–20 years of experience
