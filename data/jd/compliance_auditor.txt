Compliance Auditor
Job Summary:
Ensure that organizational processes comply with regulatory, legal, and internal standards
through systematic audits and assessments.
Key Responsibilities:
• Conduct compliance audits across functions
• Evaluate adherence to regulatory and legal requirements
• Identify risks and non-compliance issues
• Prepare audit reports and recommend corrective actions
• Monitor implementation of compliance measures
• Support regulatory inspections and reviews
Required Skills:
• Knowledge of compliance frameworks and regulations
• Risk assessment and auditing skills
• Strong analytical and documentation skills
• Attention to detail
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Law, or related field
• 3–7 years of experience
