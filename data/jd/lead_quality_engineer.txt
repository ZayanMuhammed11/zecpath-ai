Lead Quality Engineer
Job Summary:
Oversee quality engineering activities, manage teams, and ensure consistent implementation
of quality standards.
Key Responsibilities:
• Lead and manage quality engineering teams
• Develop and enforce quality strategies and policies
• Review and approve quality plans and procedures
• Drive continuous improvement and defect reduction initiatives
• Collaborate with stakeholders across departments
Required Skills:
• Team leadership and management
• Strong decision-making abilities
• Expertise in quality systems and standards
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering
• 6–10 years of experience
