Food Quality Engineer
Job Summary:
Ensure that food products meet quality, safety, and regulatory standards throughout
production, processing, and distribution.
Key Responsibilities:
• Develop and implement food quality standards and procedures
• Monitor production processes to ensure compliance with food safety regulations
• Conduct inspections, sampling, and testing of raw materials and finished products
• Analyze quality issues and implement corrective/preventive actions (CAPA)
• Ensure compliance with regulatory standards (FSSAI, ISO 22000, HACCP)
• Collaborate with production, R&D, and supply chain teams
• Maintain quality documentation and reports
Required Skills:
• Knowledge of food safety standards and regulations
• Quality control and inspection techniques
• Problem-solving and analytical skills
• Attention to detail
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Food Technology, Food Science, or related field
• 2–6 years of experience in food or FMCG industry
