Aerospace Quality Engineer
Job Summary:
Ensure that aerospace products, components, and processes meet stringent quality, safety, and
regulatory standards throughout the product lifecycle.
Key Responsibilities:
• Implement and maintain aerospace quality standards and procedures
• Conduct product and process audits
• Perform root cause analysis and implement corrective/preventive actions (CAPA)
• Ensure compliance with aerospace standards and customer requirements
• Support new product development and qualification processes
• Monitor quality metrics (defects, non-conformance, reliability)
• Collaborate with design, manufacturing, and supplier teams
Required Skills:
• Knowledge of aerospace quality standards and regulations
• Familiarity with AS9100, FAA, and EASA requirements
• Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
• Experience with quality tools (FMEA, SPC, 8D)
• Attention to detail and documentation expertise
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Aerospace, Mechanical, or related Engineering field
• 3–8 years of experience in aerospace or high-precision manufacturing
