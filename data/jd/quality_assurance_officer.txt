Quality Assurance Officer (Food
Industry)
Job Summary:
Ensure that food production processes consistently meet quality and safety standards through
monitoring, auditing, and compliance activities.
Key Responsibilities:
• Monitor production processes and ensure adherence to quality standards
• Conduct internal audits and inspections
• Maintain quality and food safety documentation
• Handle customer complaints and quality issues
• Support regulatory audits and certifications
• Coordinate with production and quality teams for issue resolution
• Ensure compliance with FSSAI and international standards
Required Skills:
• Knowledge of food safety and quality systems
• Strong documentation and auditing skills
• Attention to detail
• Communication and coordination skills
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Food Technology, Food Science, or related field
• 2–5 years of experience in food manufacturing or FMCG industry
