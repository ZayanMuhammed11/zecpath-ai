Internal Quality Auditor
Job Summary:
Conduct internal audits to evaluate the effectiveness of the Quality Management System
(QMS) and ensure compliance with organizational and regulatory standards.
Key Responsibilities:
• Plan and execute internal audits across departments
• Evaluate compliance with QMS, policies, and procedures
• Identify non-conformities and areas for improvement
• Prepare audit reports and communicate findings
• Follow up on corrective and preventive actions (CAPA)
• Support external audits and certification processes
Required Skills:
• Knowledge of auditing principles and techniques
• Familiarity with ISO standards (ISO 9001, etc.)
• Strong analytical and reporting skills
• Attention to detail
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Quality Management, or related field
• 2–5 years of experience
• Internal Auditor certification preferred
