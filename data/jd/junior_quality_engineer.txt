Junior Quality Engineer

Job Summary:
Support quality assurance and control activities to ensure products meet defined standards
and customer requirements.
Key Responsibilities:
• Assist in implementing quality control processes and procedures
• Conduct inspections and basic testing of products
• Record and analyze quality data
• Support root cause analysis for defects
• Maintain quality documentation and reports
• Coordinate with production and engineering teams
Required Skills:
• Basic knowledge of quality tools (7 QC tools)
• Understanding of manufacturing processes
• Analytical and problem-solving skills
• Attention to detail
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering (Mechanical, Industrial, or related)
• 0–2 years of experience
