Environmental Test Engineer
Job Summary:
Ensure product reliability and durability by conducting environmental and stress testing.
Key Responsibilities:
• Design and perform environmental tests (temperature, humidity, vibration, etc.)
• Analyze test data and identify failure modes
• Ensure compliance with environmental and industry standards
• Prepare test reports and documentation
• Collaborate with design and quality teams for improvements
Required Skills:
• Knowledge of environmental testing standards
• Data analysis and failure analysis
• Understanding of product reliability concepts
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering
• 2–6 years of experience
