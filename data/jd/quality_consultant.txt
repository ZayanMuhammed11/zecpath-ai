Quality Consultant
Job Summary:
Provide expert guidance to organizations on improving quality systems, processes, and
overall operational performance.
Key Responsibilities:
• Assess current quality systems and identify improvement areas
• Develop and implement quality strategies and frameworks
• Conduct audits and gap analysis
• Recommend and implement best practices
• Train teams on quality tools and methodologies
• Support certification and compliance initiatives
Required Skills:
• Strong knowledge of quality management systems (QMS)
• Problem-solving and analytical skills
• Consulting and client management abilities
• Communication and training skills
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s/Master’s degree in Engineering or Management
• 8–15 years of experience in quality roles
