Production Quality Specialist
Job Summary:
Ensure consistent product quality during production by monitoring operations and resolving
quality issues in real time.
Key Responsibilities:
• Monitor production quality and performance metrics
• Conduct in-process inspections and checks
• Identify defects and coordinate corrective actions
• Work closely with production teams to maintain quality standards
• Maintain quality documentation and reports
Required Skills:
• Production process knowledge
• Quality inspection and control techniques
• Attention to detail and problem-solving
Qualifications:
• Diploma or Bachelor’s degree in Engineering
• 2–5 years of experience
