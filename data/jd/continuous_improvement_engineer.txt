Continuous Improvement Engineer

Drive ongoing process improvements to enhance efficiency, quality, and productivity.
Key Responsibilities:
• Identify improvement opportunities in processes
• Implement Lean and Six Sigma methodologies
• Analyze process performance data
• Lead improvement projects and initiatives
• Monitor and sustain improvements
Required Skills:
• Lean manufacturing and Six Sigma knowledge
• Data analysis and problem-solving
• Process optimization skills
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering or related field
• 3–7 years of experience
