Verification Engineer
Job Summary:
Verify that products and systems meet design specifications and functional requirements.
Key Responsibilities:
• Develop verification plans and test cases
• Execute functional and performance verification tests
• Analyze test results and identify deviations
• Collaborate with design and development teams
• Ensure traceability between requirements and test results
• Document verification outcomes and reports
Required Skills:
• Knowledge of verification methodologies
• Test planning and execution
• Analytical thinking and troubleshooting
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering (Mechanical, Electrical, Software, or related)
• 2–5 years of experience
