Quality Analytics Engineer
Job Summary:
Use advanced analytics to derive insights from quality data and drive process improvements.
Key Responsibilities:
• Analyze quality data using statistical and analytical tools
• Develop dashboards and reports for quality metrics
• Identify trends, patterns, and improvement opportunities
• Support data-driven decision-making
• Collaborate with cross-functional teams
Required Skills:
• Data analytics (Python, R, SQL)
• Statistical analysis
• Visualization tools (Power BI, Tableau)
• Problem-solving skills
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Data Analytics, or related field
• 3–7 years of experience
