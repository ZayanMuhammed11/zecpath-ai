Manufacturing Quality Engineer
Job Summary:
Ensure product quality throughout the manufacturing process by monitoring production,
implementing quality systems, and driving continuous improvement.
Key Responsibilities:
• Develop and implement manufacturing quality standards and procedures
• Monitor production processes to ensure adherence to quality requirements
• Conduct root cause analysis and implement corrective/preventive actions (CAPA)
• Perform process audits and inspections
• Collaborate with production, design, and supply chain teams
• Drive defect reduction and process optimization initiatives
Required Skills:
• Knowledge of manufacturing processes
• Quality tools (SPC, FMEA, Control Plans)
• Analytical and problem-solving skills
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical, Industrial, or Manufacturing Engineering
• 2–6 years of experience
