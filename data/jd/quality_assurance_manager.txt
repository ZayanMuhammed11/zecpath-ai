Quality Assurance Manager
Job Summary:
Ensure that processes and systems consistently deliver products meeting defined quality
standards.
Key Responsibilities:
• Establish QA systems and procedures
• Conduct internal and external audits
• Ensure process compliance with standards (ISO 9001, etc.)
• Manage documentation and quality records
• Lead continuous improvement initiatives
Required Skills:
• Strong auditing and documentation skills
• Knowledge of ISO standards
• Process improvement expertise
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering or related field
• 8–12 years of experience
