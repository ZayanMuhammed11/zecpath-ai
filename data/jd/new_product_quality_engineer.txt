New Product Quality Engineer (NPQE)
Job Summary:
Ensure quality readiness for new product development and successful product launches.
Key Responsibilities:
• Define quality requirements for new products
• Support new product introduction (NPI/NPD) processes
• Develop control plans and inspection criteria
• Coordinate cross-functional teams during product launch
• Identify risks and implement mitigation strategies
• Ensure smooth transition from development to production
Required Skills:
• New Product Development (NPD) and NPI processes
• APQP fundamentals
• Risk management and problem-solving
• Cross-functional coordination
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering
• 3–7 years of experience
