Lean Quality Engineer
Job Summary:
Integrate Lean principles with quality engineering to eliminate waste and improve process
efficiency.
Key Responsibilities:
• Implement Lean tools (5S, Value Stream Mapping, Kanban)
• Identify and eliminate non-value-added activities
• Improve process flow and efficiency
• Support continuous improvement initiatives
• Train teams on Lean methodologies
Required Skills:
• Lean manufacturing expertise
• Process mapping and analysis
• Strong problem-solving skills
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering
• 3–7 years of experience
• Lean certification preferred
