 Plant Quality Manager
Job Summary:
Ensure quality across all plant operations and manufacturing processes.
Key Responsibilities:
• Oversee plant-level quality operations
• Monitor production quality metrics
• Ensure compliance with manufacturing standards
• Lead quality audits and inspections
• Drive defect reduction and process improvements
Required Skills:
• Manufacturing and process knowledge
• Leadership and team management
• Quality tools expertise
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering
• 10–15 years of experience
