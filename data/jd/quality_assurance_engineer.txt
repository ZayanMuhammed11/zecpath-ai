Quality Assurance Engineer
Job Summary:
Ensure that systems, processes, and procedures consistently produce quality outputs.
Key Responsibilities:
• Design and implement QA systems and procedures
• Conduct internal audits and compliance checks
• Monitor process adherence to standards
• Drive continuous improvement initiatives
• Maintain documentation and quality records
Required Skills:
• ISO 9001 knowledge
• Auditing and documentation expertise
• Process improvement mindset
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering or related field
• 2–5 years of experience
