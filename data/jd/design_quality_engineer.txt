Design Quality Engineer
Job Summary:
Ensure that product designs meet quality, reliability, and regulatory requirements from
concept through production.
Key Responsibilities:
• Integrate quality requirements into product design and development
• Conduct design reviews and risk assessments (DFMEA)
• Ensure compliance with industry standards and specifications
• Collaborate with design, R&D, and manufacturing teams
• Analyze design-related failures and implement improvements
• Support validation and verification activities
Required Skills:
• Design for Quality (DFQ) principles
• DFMEA and risk analysis
• Understanding of product lifecycle management (PLM)
• Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical, Electrical, or related Engineering field
• 3–7 years of experience
