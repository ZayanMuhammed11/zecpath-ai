Advanced Product Quality Planning
(APQP) Engineer
Job Summary:
Lead and manage APQP processes to ensure product quality during development and launch
phases.
Key Responsibilities:
• Plan and execute APQP activities
• Develop and review key deliverables (DFMEA, PFMEA, Control Plans, PPAP)
• Coordinate with cross-functional teams and suppliers
• Monitor project timelines and quality milestones
• Ensure compliance with customer and industry standards
• Support production readiness and launch activities
Required Skills:
• Strong knowledge of APQP and PPAP processes
• Risk assessment and mitigation
• Project management skills
• Knowledge of automotive or manufacturing standards
Qualifications:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering
• 4–8 years of experience
• Experience in automotive or manufacturing industries preferred
