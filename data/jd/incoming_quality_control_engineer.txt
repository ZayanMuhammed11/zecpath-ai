Incoming Quality Control (IQC) Engineer

Job Summary:
Inspect incoming raw materials and components to ensure they meet quality standards.
Key Responsibilities:
• Perform incoming inspections
• Approve or reject materials
• Maintain inspection records
• Communicate with suppliers for defects
• Ensure compliance with specifications
Required Skills:
• Knowledge of inspection techniques
• Understanding of material specifications
• Attention to detail
Qualifications:
• Diploma/Bachelor’s degree in Engineering
• 0–2 years experience
